# Dashboard

The dashboard shows the state of your API keys and cost centers at the top, followed by **Budget and usage** for a selectable **Month**.

## API keys

- The top line shows how many of your keys are active.
- Without a key you see a hint; **Create new key** creates your first key right there.
- Hints appear for blocked and expired keys and for keys expiring within the next 14 days. Otherwise it says **All keys are active.**
- **Go to API keys** opens the key list.

## Cost centers

- The cost centers you can create keys on, with your role (**Member** or **Cost center admin**). The default cost center is always included.
- Open join requests are shown as **Requested**.
- **Join a cost center** opens the list of all approved cost centers.

## Budget and usage

- **Assigned budget**: amount and period (monthly, yearly or project) that an admin assigned to you. Without a budget you can only use free models.
- **Spend in period**: total cost of all your keys in the period.
- **Remaining budget** and **Utilization**: how much of the budget is left. The bar turns yellow at 80 % and red at 100 %.
- **Total requests**, **Successful**, **Failed**, **Avg. cost per request** and **Total tokens** (input and output).

## Charts and tables

- **Daily spend**: cost per day in the selected month.
- **Last 12 months**: monthly spend history.
- **Top keys**, **Top models**, **Spend by provider**: breakdown of your usage.

## Budget exhausted

Once your spend reaches 100 % of the budget, all your keys are blocked automatically and a red banner appears. The block ends when a new budget period starts or an admin raises the budget. You receive an e-mail at 80 % and at 100 %.
