# Budget and usage

The budget screen shows your usage for a selectable month.

## Metrics

- **Assigned budget**: amount and period (monthly, yearly or project) that an admin assigned to you. Without a budget you can only use free models.
- **Spend**: total cost of all your keys in the period.
- **Remaining** and **Utilization**: how much of the budget is left. The bar turns yellow at 80 % and red at 100 %.
- **Requests**: total, successful, failed; plus average cost per request and total tokens.

## Charts and tables

- **Daily spend**: cost per day in the selected month.
- **Last 12 months**: monthly spend history.
- **Top keys**, **Top models**, **Spend by provider**: breakdown of your usage.

## Budget exhausted

Once your spend reaches 100 % of the budget, all your keys are blocked automatically and a red banner appears. The block ends when a new budget period starts or an admin raises the budget. You receive an e-mail at 80 % and at 100 %.
