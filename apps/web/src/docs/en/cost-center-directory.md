# Cost centers

All approved cost centers, searchable by name. Your own cost centers are listed on top; the default cost center is always included. The cost center number is not shown here. The search filters all entries by name.

- **Status**: your role (member or cost center admin), **Requested** for an open request, or **Rejected** (reason in the tooltip).
- **Join**: **Send request** sends a join request, with a **Message (optional)** (e.g. project or reason). The owner and the cost center admins receive an e-mail and decide; admins can decide as well.
- Once approved, you are a member, get an e-mail and can create keys on the cost center.
- After a rejection you get the reason by e-mail and can request again.
